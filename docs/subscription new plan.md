## Additional Updates To Be Made

### Shared Operations
> Workers should be able to open more than 1 cart at a time so they can serve more than 1 customer at once.
> There should be separate buttons to register tables or rooms under their specific sections.

Hospitality / hotels
> Hospitality should support hall bookings with time, sitting capacity, decoration options, and other hall features/services.
> Hotels should be able to customize worker access by role, such as receptionist, waiter, manager, HR, etc.
> Hospitality and hotels should support pool bookings by number of persons and number of hours.
> In hotel, kitchen, or lounge accounts/pages, inventory should mean the goods available for sale, such as food, drinks, and related items.
> All hotel businesses should have inventory where food and drinks can be added to the menu and also attached to room billing.

Kitchen / restaurant / lounge operations
> Kitchens should be able to choose table sizes such as tables for 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, or 10.
> Kitchen, bar, and lounge orders should be attachable to a table, remain under cart/invoice even after being served, and proceed to checkout only when the table is closed and payment is made.
> Rice and stew, or swallow and soup, should be treated as 1 meal, with options to add different soups that go with the meal during meal setup/registration.
> Kitchen should be able to add drinks like water, Coke, and similar items to inventory, but access should still be controlled by tier limits.
> While carting orders in kitchen, the cart section should include extras/options such as take-away status, quantity of meat, and similar add-ons.

Pricing / tier structure
> Kitchen and restaurant tiers should pay the same pricing as retail tiers.
> Only hospitality and lounge should have different pricing from the rest, because lounge combines bar and kitchen, while hospitality combines bar, kitchen, rooms, hall, pool, and related services.
